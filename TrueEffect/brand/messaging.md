# Messaging

## Core Lines

- "When the world changes, know what it means for you."
- "News tells you what happened. TrueEffect tells you what it means for your life."
- "Your personal radar for real-world change."
- "Not more headlines. Real-life meaning."
- "Understand what affects your money, family, home, job, safety, town, and future."

## Messaging Notes

TrueEffect should sound calm, practical, grounded, and useful. It should avoid fear, outrage, partisan cues, and vague AI hype.
